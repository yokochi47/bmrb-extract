{#- ===================================================== 9. RDC violations -#}

9. RDC violation analysis

{%- if rdc_correlations %}

The following plots and tables show the correlation between observed and calculated RDCs, based on molecular alignment tensors predicted using the coordinates from each saveframe. A high linear correlation (e.g., r² > 0.9) indicates that the RDCs are consistent with the coordinates. Conversely, a low correlation (e.g., r² < 0.8) suggests that the molecular alignment tensor was not accurately determined; possible reasons for this include the presence of RDCs arising from flexible regions, an overestimation of scaling factors for unreliable RDC, or the molecule inherently adopting multiple conformations. A lower Q score (e.g. Q < 0.2) indicates a better structural fit, whereas a high Q score (e.g. Q > 0.3) suggests significant structural errors or incorrect stereochemical configurations. 'Q score (Clore)' is preferable in case of a limited RDC sampling over all possible orientations.
