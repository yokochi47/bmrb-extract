1. Introduction

The bmrb_extract service ("Service") provides automated tools for conversion and processing of NMR and related structural data. The Service is intended to support the scientific community in preparing, validating, and exchanging data with best practices adopted by the Worldwide Protein Data Bank (wwPDB).

To use this Service, you must agree to these Terms and Conditions ("Terms") and Privacy Policy.

2. Scientific Purpose and Scope
  • Facilitate preparation and conversion of NMR and related structural data
  • Support workflows consistent with existing deposition systems such as OneDep and BMRBdep
  • Promote interoperability with community standard formats; NMR-STAR or NEF (if possible) for NMR data, PDBx/mmCIF for coordinates, respectively

The Service is intended for research and scientific use only.

7. Upload File Check

Users receive one of three conversion status based on analysis of the uploaded files:

  • OK – No issue found and users can download the conversion results, including model file, NMR data file, and report file
  • Warning – Minor issues were found during the conversion, but you can access the conversion results by accepting all warnings or try to fix the issues reported before download the results
  • Error – Failed in conversion due to format issue in uploaded files, inconsistencies among uploaded files (e.g. model vs NMR data) or missing mandatory contents. Users can achieve the desired results by re-uploading files
